# Context lenses, not institutional policy packs

Read the relevant rows after the PM selects a profile. Use them to discover missing decisions and actual owners, not to add predetermined requirements. Different sectors share failure shapes; their answers do not transfer. A sector label is not evidence of a legal rule, internal standard, risk appetite or deployed capability.

| Lens | Questions that change the scope or implementation |
|---|---|
| financial | When does an effect become final? Can retry duplicate it? What reconciles partial completion? |
| identity | Who acts for whom? What is rechecked? What happens after authority changes? |
| privacy | Which data is needed, exposed, retained and removed? Who supplies the binding policy? |
| campaign | Which eligibility instant, version, quota and overlap rule apply? What if configuration changes mid-flow? |
| async | Who owns pending state, timeout, late completion, ordering and duplicate delivery? |
| integration | Which actual interface/version exists? Who owns each side and failure recovery? |
| migration | What preserves old meaning? How are partial backfills, coexistence and rollback handled? |
| channels | What differs between web, app, assisted channels and older clients? |
| reporting | What is the metric's definition, event time, correction rule and reconciliation source? |
| operations | How is failure detected, assigned, supported, retried or reversed? |

## Fictional contrast: lending and digital channels

A lending request may activate financial, identity, privacy and integration lenses. That prompts questions about commitment, customer/representative authority, underwriting interfaces and decision ownership. It does not supply an approval threshold, retention period or legal requirement.

A digital-channel campaign may activate campaign, channels, async and privacy lenses. That prompts questions about eligibility timing, repeated enrollment, quota changes and app/web differences. It does not assert that a telecom company uses a particular backend or fraud rule.

For either, create stakeholder consultations only when a concrete unanswered question needs an owner. The PM gathers their views; the guide records the supplied evidence and the PM's interpretation. Never impersonate the department or auto-send a consultation.

## Work shape before ceremony

An exact copy replacement may need only request, scope, acceptance and handoff if evidence explicitly excludes changed interactions, data, contracts and risk-bearing behavior. A UI decision with changed navigation is not merely copy work. A backend-only request can exclude design; it cannot skip its actual flow or required interface decisions. A migration may have no screen but still require a complete data lifecycle, compatibility contract, rollback questions and regression surface.

The profile is a reviewed proposal, not a risk score. Revisit it when evidence changes. If its exclusions would hide a discovered dependency, return that conflict to the PM; without an answer, keep delivery blocked.
