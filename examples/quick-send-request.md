## Request: Quick Send

**From:** Retail Banking — Digital Channels
**Date:** 12 August · **Priority:** High

### The ask

We would like a "Quick Send" area added to our mobile app. On the home screen the customer sees the people they last sent money to, and can send again with a single tap.

Three competitor banks already have something similar. Our satisfaction surveys include comments that the money transfer flow is too long. We expect this to shorten the transfer journey and increase transfer volume through the mobile channel.

### Flow

1. Customer opens the app and sees the "Quick Send" area on the home screen
2. The people they last sent money to are listed
3. Customer taps a person
4. Amount is entered and the transfer completes

It is important that the flow is as short as possible. The current transfer journey has too many steps; here the customer should get a fast experience without being asked for a password. Our existing security steps will of course be preserved.

### Scope

International transfers and foreign currency are out of scope for this phase. Only local currency, transfers within our bank and to other banks.

The existing money transfer infrastructure will be used; no new service development is expected.

### Design

We are thinking of a horizontally scrollable card area on the home screen. The UX team will do the design. A smooth, modern user experience is important to us.

### Timing

We are targeting go-live by the end of Q3, active for the Q4 campaign period.
